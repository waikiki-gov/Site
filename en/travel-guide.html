Before you go

  1. Check your entry route

    Citizens of American Union, EU and partner states travel visa-free for up to 30 days. Everyone else applies for an e-Visa through the Waikiki One portal.

  2. Passport and insurance

    Your passport must be valid for six months after arrival. Visitors need travel health insurance; state healthcare is free for citizens only.

  3. Plan provincial trips

    Rainforest expeditions in Amazonia require vaccination and a licensed guide; protected reserves admit a limited number of visitors per day.

  4. Download Waikiki One

    The visitor mode stores your e-Visa, transit passes, museum tickets and payments in one secure app.

Getting there and getting around

Waikiki stretches from the Caribbean to the Amazon, yet a modern network keeps every province within easy reach.

By air

Nova Aurelia International is the main gateway, with direct flights from every continent. São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro and Manaus serve the mainland provinces.

By rail from Florida

The Florida-Waikiki undersea corridor brings maglev services from the United States directly to the islands.

By sea

Cruise terminals in Nova Aurelia, Port Royal and other ports welcome ocean liners and private yachts throughout the year.

On the ground

Metro, trams and buses

Urban public transport is available in every major city. Metro lines, electric trams and buses run around the clock.

Hyperloop

Reach Mega Pyramid City from central Nova Aurelia in minutes on the world's first public Hyperloop line.

Cable cars and elevated rail

An elevated railway serves Palm Island; cable cars and bridges link the islands of the World Islands archipelago.

Water taxis

Electric water taxis connect marinas, island resorts and the waterfront, the only way to reach some of the World Islands.

When to visit

The Caribbean dry season from December to April brings cloudless skies and the year's great festivals. Amazonia is most accessible in the drier months, while the Andes ski season peaks from June to August.

Smart travel and digital living

Waikiki is the most technologically advanced nation on Earth. Seamless digital systems eliminate waiting times, replace paperwork, and ensure complete convenience throughout your trip.

Waikiki One Super-App

Your indispensable all-in-one travel companion. Stores your biometric e-Visa, digital hotel room key, transit tickets, museum passes, and direct access to 24/7 multilingual emergency assistance.

100% Cashless Island Economy

Paper cash is not in circulation. The Waikiki Dollar is accepted via contactless cards, phone wallets, or Waikiki One NFC anywhere from high-end boutiques to beachside fruit stands with zero foreign transaction fees.

Nationwide 6G and State Wi-Fi

Visitors might purchase an automated digital eSIM upon arrival. High-speed, encrypted public Wi-Fi is universally accessible across all metro lines, ferries, beaches, and national park visitor lodges.

Green Mobility Transit Passes

Choose an unlimited 3-day or 7-day Transit Freedom Pass. Valid on autonomous electric metros, trams, hyperloop shuttles, Florida maglev trains, and water taxis with simple tap-and-go access.

Biometric Border Clearance in 15 Seconds

Link your biometric passport in the Waikiki One app 24 hours prior to departure. Upon touchdown at Nova Aurelia International, scan your face at the Smart Gates to clear customs in under a minute without filling out paper arrival cards.

Etiquette, dining and practical tips

Essential advice for a seamless journey. From dining etiquette and Blue Belt conservation rules to dress codes, tipping, and practical island living.

Culinary Fusion

World-class chefs blend fresh Caribbean seafood with Amazonian superberries and Mediterranean recipes, creating dishes unavailable anywhere else on Earth.

Venue Dress Code

Smart dress is expected at government buildings, the Royal Opera, and fine dining venues. Reservations for signature restaurants should be booked 2 to 4 weeks in advance via Waikiki One.

Service and Tipping

All bills incorporate a fair service charge that directly rewards hospitality staff. Tipping is not required, but rounding up for exceptional care is warmly appreciated.

Protect the Blue Belt

Touching corals, taking shells, anchoring outside designated mooring buoys, and feeding wild marine life carry substantial fines under the Blue Belt Treaty. All scuba and snorkeling excursions must be accompanied by licensed conservation divemasters.

Rainforest Wilderness Permits

To prevent overtourism and preserve fragile habitats, daily visitor entries to primary Amazonian reserves are capped. Apply for your complimentary wilderness permit via the national park portal at least 7 days before your expedition.

Packing for Microclimates

Pack breathable linen and swimwear for Nova Aurelia's tropical heat, lightweight waterproof trekking gear and insect repellent for Amazonia, and warm thermal layers if visiting the Andes Ski Paradise.

Photography and Drones

Commercial drones and unauthorized aerial photography are strictly restricted around the government quarter, defense facilities and the Kennedy Space Centre.

Safety and Assistance

Waikiki is recognized as one of the safest nations in the world. 24/7 emergency medical and consular services respond through the Waikiki One app in all major languages.
