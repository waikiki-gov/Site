Chease Young's Education and First Enterprise

Chease Young was born in New York State in 1972. From childhood, he was keenly interested in economic processes. In 1987, he was admitted to Stanford University in California, where he received an economics degree with honors in 1991. Following his studies, he joined his family's hospitality business and began working as the director and owner of the luxurious Starlight Hotels inherited from his father, accumulating substantial initial wealth through the company's success.

Foreign visits

In 2021 and 2022, several foreign heads of government and state visited Waikiki to hold talks with Raimondo and Selena or other high-ranking members of the government. In January, German Chancellor Olaf Scholz traveled to Nova Aurelia to discuss the Paris Climate Agreement with Chease Young, and then in March, Raimondo and his sisters spent two weeks in Tokyo to negotiate visa-free travel between the two countries and a joint space program with Japanese Prime Minister Fumio Kishida. Chease and Jessica met with Joe Biden and Kamala Harris in Washington in July. Although diplomatic relations between the two countries deteriorated during Biden's presidency, the USA remains Waikiki's number one ally. During their European vacation in the summer, Jessica and her family also met with French President Emmanuel Macron and King Felipe VI of Spain. At the beginning of 2022, Argentine President Alberto Fernández traveled to Waikiki to ask for financial support due to increased energy prices, and with Raimondo's contribution, a long-term energy agreement beneficial to both countries was reached. Shortly after her election, the President of Hungary also received an invitation from Chease Young. The main topics of the discussions were family support and the immigration crisis affecting Europe, but energy cooperation between the two countries was also discussed.
