Princess of Waikiki

Angelina Chease

Princess of Waikiki, eldest child of the Chease dynasty, Senator, and an uncompromising advocate for a sustainable future. From green finance to the stage of Davos, from the Senate floor to the Amazon rainforest, I carry the conviction that true wealth lies not in possession, but in preservation.

👑 Princess of Waikiki | 🏛️ Senator | 💍 Wife of Taylor Lautner | 🌱 Founder of GaiaNova Holding | 🎤 Youngest Speaker at the Davos Forum | 🚀 Astronaut | 🫧 Co-founder of the Clear Water Initiative

From Washington to Nova Aurelia 🌴

My earliest memories are of my parents, Chease Young and Jessica Walker, making the boldest decision of their lives, building modern Waikiki from the remnants of the collapsed Cuban state. Overnight, I found myself in the middle of a developing country where opportunities were endless and the responsibility was immense. 🏗️ During my primary and high school years in Nova Aurelia, I turned my attention to topics beyond the standard curriculum early on, participating in Model UN conferences and exploring complex economic systems. Although the world's top foreign universities offered me scholarships, my heart drew me home, and I ultimately chose the Waikiki Economics University. 🎓

The University Where I Found Everything 📖

I earned my BSc degree in 2011 and my Master's degree in 2013 in International Economics at WEU. I insisted on being treated as an ordinary student, despite my family leading the country. I was most drawn to sustainable corporate governance and public-private partnerships. It was during a debate at the Global Futures Club that I met Taylor, who challenged me on energy policy with arguments so compelling that I immediately recognized him as my intellectual match. 💡 We spent hours continuing our discussions in the university café; what began as an intellectual alliance quickly blossomed into a deep, genuine love. 🥰 A scholarship program at the IESE Business School in Barcelona further broadened my horizons, where I attended lectures in three languages on European public services and ethical leadership.

As Senator for a Green Future 🏛️

In 2015, my father established the Senate, appointing Raimondo, Jennifer, and me among its first members. 📋 As a Senator, I specialized in monetary affairs, green finance, and investment strategies. I co-authored the Waikiki Sustainable Industry Program, which mandates a green transition in energy and construction, targeting complete carbon neutrality by 2030. The Tesla-Waikiki Green Energy Agreement also passed through my hands, which I personally negotiated with Elon Musk at the SpaceX headquarters. 🔋 During the construction of the Mega Pyramid and other major projects, I successfully lobbied to ensure that every mega-investment remains carbon neutral.

Diplomacy and the World Stage 🌍

At just 21 years old, I stood before the world's economic elite as the youngest plenary speaker at the World Economic Forum in Davos, arguing that a state's stability is defined not simply by GDP, but by the trust of its citizens. Foreign Policy Magazine promptly named me to its Top 30 Global Thinkers Under 30 list! 🏆 I represented Waikiki at the Rio Olympics, spoke in the Ottawa Parliament, participated in a roundtable discussion with Ivanka Trump at the "Women in Power" forum, and in 2021 joined my siblings on a joint diplomatic mission to Japan, laying the groundwork for space industry cooperation between our two nations. In 2018, Forbes ranked me as the world's fifth most influential person, ahead of Vladimir Putin, George Bush, and Xi Jinping. 📈

Taylor, the Partner of My Life ❤️

I didn’t meet Taylor Lautner on a red carpet or a film set, but in a university debate circle where he stood as an equal opponent. That evening changed everything. After weeks of late-night discussions, joint projects, and an inseparable friendship, he finally took the first step and asked me out. 📅 He didn’t need to see a princess in me; he simply found the person with whom he could dream of the same future. 🌿 Our first vacation together on Isla de la Estrella sealed our bond under starlit skies. Taylor was crowned Prince of Waikiki in 2017, but for me, it is not his title that makes him special, but the calm and unwavering support he offers even on my most demanding days.

Business, Environmentalism, and GaiaNova 🌳

In 2017, I founded GaiaNova, a holding company that develops green startups and decentralized energy production systems in Waikiki and South America. Our first major project was a solar panel manufacturing plant in Manaus, Amazonia, creating nearly 3,000 jobs. 🏭 I also joined the board of the Chease Investment Group, acquiring significant shares in Fortune 500 companies to help shape ethical business models. Together with Taylor, we launched a global campaign to reduce ocean plastic pollution, personally collecting waste on Waikiki's beaches, and we continue to work on promoting the Blue Belt Agreement. 🌊 For me, investing is not merely about returns, but a tool to build a better world.

Adventures from the Stars to the Rainforest 🚀

In 2021, Taylor and I spent ten days aboard the Waikiki National Space Station, where alongside our orbital research, I broadcast live, interactive lessons to Waikiki students, highlighting the fragility of our planet and the vital importance of environmental protection from the perspective of space. 🌌 Down on Earth, we also strive to stay active; with Taylor, we have traveled to places ranging from the Amazon rainforests to the endangered ecosystems of the Great Barrier Reef. I took part in the ground-breaking ceremony for the Mega Pyramid City Hyperloop line, and during the global pandemic, I played a role in coordinating vaccine procurement and public communication. 💪 Wherever duty calls, I am always searching for ways to best serve our community.

As the eldest child of the Chease dynasty, I inherited not a throne, but a mission. As long as a single coral reef perishes or a single youth is left without opportunity, my work is not finished. 🌏
