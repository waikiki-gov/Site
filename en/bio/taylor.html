Prince of Waikiki

Taylor Lautner

Prince and Senator of Waikiki, committed patron of public security, sustainable transportation, and youth sports programs. As the husband of Angelina Chease, he is an active supporter of the dynasty's green initiatives, whether preserving ocean ecosystems, leading research expeditions to the Amazon rainforest, or participating in scientific space missions.

👑 Prince of Waikiki | 🏛️ Senator | 💍 Husband of Angelina Chease | 🥋 Former Karate Champion | 🌿 Environmentalist | 🚀 Astronaut | ⚽ Founder of Waikiki's Future Champions

Hollywood and the New Home 🎬

Before dedicating my life to the development and public affairs of Waikiki, most of the world knew me from the silver screen and the red carpets of Hollywood. Alongside my acting career, discipline and martial arts defined my youth. I am proud of my success at the junior karate world championships, which taught me perseverance and self-control. 🏅 Although the glamorous world of California offered great opportunities, I felt the need for a much deeper, more creative calling. That is why I made the decision to leave the American film industry behind and move to Waikiki, using my talents and work to help build a new, ambitious nation. 🏡

Meeting at University and Joint Initiatives 🎓

The most important milestone of my years at the Waikiki Economics University was undoubtedly meeting Angelina during an autumn debate at the Global Futures Club. We stood on opposite sides of a highly exciting debate, and from our intellectual alliance, a deep love was quickly born. In the spring of 2012, we co-founded the WEU Sustainability Circle, which initiated the green modernization of the campus. ♻️ In the summer of that same year, we took our first trip together, where I taught Angelina to paddleboard in the shallow lagoons. In 2013, I accompanied her to Barcelona for her guest studies, where I worked as a cultural researcher. For our first family Christmas together, I brought the tradition of my California family and wrote a personal poem for each of our loved ones under the tree. 🎄

Senate Work and Public Security Reform 🪪

On May 18, 2015, following the historic constitutional amendment, Chease Young appointed me as a member of the newly established Waikiki Senate. As a Senator, sports development, sustainable infrastructure, and public security were among my main responsibilities. In 2015, under my leadership, we developed a comprehensive reform of the Waikiki law enforcement system, focusing on digitalization and community policing. This modernization package was approved by the citizens with an overwhelming majority in a 2016 national referendum. 🗳️ In addition, I advocated for sustainable public transportation projects aimed at connecting our major cities in an environmentally friendly way and achieving carbon-neutral mobility. 🚋

Princely Status and Marriage to Angelina 💍

As the highest recognition of my service to the state, I was solemnly crowned Prince on October 16, 2017, in Nova Aurelia, formally joining the royal family. 🤴 Over the years, my relationship with Angelina grew even closer, culminating in our marriage on October 12, 2024. The wedding was held in the beachfront wing of the Palazzo Versace, over a glass altar at sunset, in the presence of two hundred of our closest relatives and friends. It was a special and moving moment when Chease Young himself walked Angelina down the aisle, and I received her with the white peony boutonniere she had chosen on my lapel. 👰 During the ceremony, we read vows we had secretly written for each other.

Environmental Protection, Space Mission, and Expeditions 🚀

Alongside my public duties, environmental protection is my greatest calling. Together with Angelina, we launched a global campaign against ocean plastic pollution, and personally participated in cleaning up Waikiki's beaches. In 2021, we shared an extraordinary adventure spending ten days aboard the Waikiki National Space Station, reaching it via a Falcon 9 rocket. 🛰️ In the spring of 2024, in partnership with WWF and Greenpeace, we led a research expedition deep into the Amazon, mapping the nesting sites of endangered macaws and various plant species. 🦜 Combining my love for sports with support for the youth, I established the Future Champions scholarship, which supports talented local young athletes in their international training.

In both sports and public life, I believe that real results are achieved through discipline, empathy, and responsibility for our environment. Waikiki's green future is our shared mission. 🌿
